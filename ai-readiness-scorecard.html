What It Measures: Five Evaluated Dimensions

RaiseMark evaluates district readiness across 125 structured assessment points (25 points per dimension). Each dimension addresses a concrete operational area where unmanaged AI introduces student privacy exposure or institutional friction.

Dimension 01 25 Points

Strategy & Leadership

Evaluates institutional vision, cabinet-level sponsorship, cross-functional oversight, board communication readiness, and the ethical principles directing district AI adoption.

Dimension 02 25 Points

Infrastructure & Technology

Reviews enterprise architecture, network filtering resilience against consumer AI endpoints, single sign-on (SSO) identity enforcement, device parity, and vendor API security.

Dimension 03 25 Points

Procurement & Financial Management

Audits software intake protocols, vetting workflows for ungoverned "free-tier" tools, student data-sharing clauses, software lifecycle management, and total cost of ownership.

Dimension 04 25 Points

Data Governance & Privacy

Examines FERPA, COPPA, and state statutory compliance, AI model training opt-outs on student work, directory information protections, and third-party vendor audit mechanisms.

Dimension 05 25 Points

Curriculum, Instruction & Staff Capacity

Assesses educator AI literacy, pedagogical integration frameworks, academic integrity guidance, human-in-the-loop validation, and differentiated professional development pipelines.

The Four Progression Tiers

Districts read numeric maturity scores as failing grades. RaiseMark deliberately reports qualitative progression tiers, providing school boards and leadership teams with an objective roadmap for growth without organizational defensiveness.

Foundational

Ad-hoc staff exploration, unvetted consumer app use on student devices, and absence of standardized board policy or procurement safeguards.

Focus: Rapid risk containment & baseline acceptable use.

Developing

Initial policy guidelines issued, early pilot software programs under review, and emerging awareness of student privacy risks among staff.

Focus: Formalizing vendor intake & security gates.

Maturing

Systemic data governance workflows, board-approved AUP with grade-band guidance, and structured educator training pathways in place.

Focus: Pedagogical integration & stack monitoring.

Mature

Continuous ecosystem surveillance, embedded instructional evaluation, active vendor compliance verification, and institutional leadership.

Focus: Sustained excellence & regional mentoring.
