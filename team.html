Our Team

Meet the experts who bridge the gap between AI potential and organizational reality. Our team combines deep technical skills with strategic foresight and behavioral science.

Dwight Jones

Founder & President

Former Colorado Commissioner of Education and Superintendent of Denver Public Schools and Clark County School District (Las Vegas). Dwight spearheaded the nationally recognized Colorado Growth Model, now adopted in over 15 states.

With deep expertise in state standards, institutional accountability, and narrowing achievement gaps, Dwight serves as Senior Advisor to Whiteboard Advisors and leads Education Partners in delivering strategic governance support to school districts nationwide.

Don Peterson

Principal Consultant

Former Chairman of the Kansas Board of Law Examiners and veteran administrative legal counsel. Decades of legal governance experience navigating complex regulatory transformations, including overseeing the comprehensive rewriting of bar examination policies and procedures as licensing shifted to modern digital standards.

Holding an M.A. in philosophy alongside his legal background, Don guides educational leadership teams through the structural, contractual, and student privacy challenges arising from AI integration.

Andrew Gitner

Chief Strategist

Founding Educator at CoGrader and K-12 Instructional Strategist. Operating at the intersection of pedagogy and Python, Andrew architected one of education's leading teacher-facing AI workflows, bringing rare technical fluency regarding the capabilities, prompt architectures, and strict statistical boundaries of Large Language Models.

With a decade of classroom teaching experience across public and charter schools, Andrew helps superintendents and tech directors evaluate classroom tooling, identify shadow AI, and structure verifiable policy controls.
