The Question It Answers

“What AI tools are currently inside our district, who is using them, and where is our student data exposed?”

Before a district can adopt an enforceable Acceptable Use Policy or evaluate expensive AI vendor contracts, leadership must establish baseline visibility. In typical district environments, central IT oversees fewer than half of the AI tools actively accessed in classrooms. The remainder operates in the background — ungoverned browser extensions, personal teacher accounts, consumer chatbots, and mobile utilities that bypass procurement reviews.

The AI 360 Review (our Compass stage) replaces guesswork with empirical inventory. We do not sell software, and we do not grade districts with punitive labels. We give superintendents, CTOs, and boards the clear signal needed to govern with confidence.

Evaluation Methodology

The CUEI-AI Scoring Framework

Every AI application, plugin, and platform detected in your district is scored across six institutional dimensions (100 points total) to deliver objective, actionable determinations.

Cost / Value 20 Pts

Total cost of ownership, seat license utilization, redundant software spend, and commercial terms relative to educational outcomes.

Utilization 20 Pts

Actual telemetry data across campuses: active student/staff adoption, depth of usage, and dependency across key grade bands.

Efficacy 20 Pts

Pedagogical validity, alignment with academic standards, cognitive offloading risk, and verified impact on instructional goals.

Privacy 20 Pts

FERPA, COPPA, and state data privacy compliance, model training on student data, data retention windows, and vendor sharing clauses.

Policy Alignment 10 Pts

Consistency with board policies, current Acceptable Use Policies (AUP), and state-level legislative AI disclosure mandates.

Transparency 10 Pts

Model documentation, algorithmic bias auditing, disclosure of underlying foundation models, and explainability for staff and parents.

Actionable Portfolio Determinations

Scores translate directly into four concrete administrative recommendations:

RETAIN

High efficacy and vetted privacy compliance. Maintain enterprise contract and support continued adoption.

RIGHT-SIZE

Valuable capability with poor license utilization. Renegotiate seat counts or consolidate redundant contracts.

SUNSET

Low utilization, redundant capabilities, or unfavorable renewal terms. Structured phase-out with minimal classroom disruption.

DISCOURAGE

Unacceptable student privacy risks, model training exposure, or harmful pedagogical profile. Block at network edge.
